# RHM for macOS

**[Download RHM](https://github.com/2005ko2020-ai/rhm-releases/releases/latest/download/RHM.zip)** (always the newest version)

## Install (one time)

1. Click the download link above and open the downloaded `RHM.zip` to unzip it.
2. Drag **RHM** into your **Applications** folder. It must be in Applications to update itself.
3. Open it the first time:
   - **macOS 14 or older:** right-click RHM → **Open** → **Open**.
   - **macOS 15 or newer:** double-click RHM, click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to RHM.
4. Allow **Screen Recording** and **Accessibility** when RHM asks.

## Updates

RHM updates itself. It checks when it opens and every few hours, then asks you to restart. Your flashcards and study history stay.

Works on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs, macOS 12 or newer.
